Our Simple Promise

Vencha Out Virtual lets your whole family enjoy Jamaican excursions together. While your walking companions explore the steep trails, you sit back in a comfortable, shaded lounge and watch live in 360° virtual reality, talking to them the whole way!

Who is Vencha Out designed for?

Using a wheelchair or scooter
Walking with a cane, crutches, or walker
Bad knees, joint pain, or steep hill fatigue
Seniors & elders who can't do long climbs

How It Works

3 Easy Steps to Enjoy the Day Together

1

Arrive Together at the Base

Everyone checks in together at our shaded, comfortable outdoor lounge at the foot of the hills.

2

Put on the Headset

While family and friends head up the trail, you put on a lightweight VR headset that connects live to the tour guide's camera.

3

See It & Talk Live

Look around in 360 degrees as if you're right there at the top, and chat live with your family the whole way!
